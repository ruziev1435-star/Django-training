My Top 3 Anime

Bleach

Manga by Tite Kubo (2001-2016) · Anime by Studio Pierrot (2004-2012, returned in 2022)

Bleach follows Ichigo Kurosaki, a teenager who can see ghosts. After meeting the Soul Reaper Rukia Kuchiki, he takes on her powers and becomes a Soul Reaper himself, protecting the living from evil spirits called Hollows and guiding lost souls to the afterlife. The story grows into a large conflict involving the Soul Society and powerful enemies.

My anime journey started with Bleach, and I immediately fell in love with its comedy and dynamic battles. Still, I believe some moments were stretched out too long, while others weren't developed well enough.

My Youth Romantic Comedy Is Wrong, As I Expected

Light novels by Wataru Watari (2011-2019) · Anime by Brain's Base and Feel (2013-2020)

Also known as Oregairu, this story follows Hachiman Hikigaya, a cynical high school student who distrusts the idea of youth and friendship. After an essay he writes worries his teacher, he is forced to join the Volunteer Service Club with the sharp-tongued Yukino Yukinoshita and later the cheerful Yui Yuigahama, where they help other students with their problems.

I had been watching some romcoms, and they felt rushed and illogical. This one makes a lot more sense, and the main protagonist's observations, his evolution, and his interactions with other people are very interesting to watch.

Re:Zero - Starting Life in Another World

Light novels by Tappei Nagatsuki (2014-present) · Anime by White Fox (2016-present)

Subaru Natsuki, a shut-in teenager, is suddenly summoned to a fantasy world. There he discovers he has the ability to "Return by Death": whenever he dies, he goes back in time to a previous point. He uses it to protect the people he cares about, including the half-elf Emilia, while facing the pain and trauma each loop brings.

I don't really know much about this anime because I was very young when I started watching it, so I don't remember much. Some day, when I was more mature, I decided to watch it again. I'm not a fan of sentimental moments in anime, but this one felt one of a kind. I don't think there is an anime similar to this one in character writing and narrative.
